# Why RoboCaps is the Future of Robotic Perception

Perception for robotics should output structure: which parts exist, where they are, and how confident we are—fast enough to act, and clear enough to trust. RoboCaps centers structure with Capsule Networks, while leveraging modern transformers and edge runtimes.

## Structure Over Labels
Boxes and labels are a start; compositional understanding is the goal. Capsules encode part–whole and pose, improving robustness in cluttered or occluded scenes.

## Pose as a First-Class Output
SE(2)/SE(3) outputs align directly to control frames. Equivariance-aware losses reduce data demands for viewpoint generalization, and pose stability accelerates grasp and QA decisions.

## Explainability that Matters
Routing attention and pose overlays provide operator-readable explanations. Audit trails support compliance and continuous improvement.

## Platform, Not a Point Tool
APIs, events, artifacts, observability, and Helm distribution make RoboCaps easy to adopt at scale. Capsule heads integrate with standard backbones, minimizing change risk.
