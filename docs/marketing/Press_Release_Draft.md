RoboCaps Launches Cognitive Perception Layer for Robotics

[City], [Date] — RoboCaps announced the availability of its structured part–pose perception platform designed for industrial QA and robotic manipulation. By reintroducing Capsule Networks with modern attention and edge deployment, RoboCaps delivers fast, interpretable perception with enterprise-grade governance.

“Perception should be actionable and explainable,” said the RoboCaps founding team. “RoboCaps turns pixels into parts and poses, with overlays and audit trails that frontline teams can trust.”

Availability: Helm chart and API are available today for pilot deployments.

About RoboCaps: RoboCaps builds the cognitive perception layer for robotics, combining attention-routed capsules, modern backbones, and edge deployment.
