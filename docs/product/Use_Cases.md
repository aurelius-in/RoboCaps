# Use Cases

## Factory QA
- Part verification and defect detection with pose-grounded overlays
- Integrations: ROS2/MQTT, Kafka events, S3 artifacts

## Robot Manipulation
- Grasping and assembly via part–pose and affordances
- Integrations: ROS2, MoveIt2, planner/controller interfaces

## Logistics
- Bin-picking and parcel QA; pose aids singulation and placement

## Healthcare (regulated)
- Instrument tracking with audit trails; subject to compliance review
