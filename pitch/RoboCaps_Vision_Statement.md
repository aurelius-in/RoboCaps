# RoboCaps Vision Statement

We believe robots should perceive the world as structured, actionable geometry — not just pixels. RoboCaps exists to make pose-aware, interpretable perception the default for automation.

Principles:
- Structure First: parts, poses, and calibrated confidence
- Edge Ready: decisions where they matter, with audit trails
- Open Interfaces: easy to integrate, observable, and secure

RoboCaps will become the cognitive perception layer for robots across industries, translating pixels into parts and poses — reliably, at the edge, and at scale.
